## 🌟 Overview

**VidsAI** is a sophisticated AI-powered platform that transforms your ideas into professional video advertisements in minutes. Whether you're creating marketing content, educational videos, or social media ads, VidsAI automates the entire video creation pipeline with cutting-edge AI technology.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎯 **Core Functionality**
- **AI-Powered Scene Generation** - Automatically creates video scenes from text scripts
- **Multi-Language Support** - Supports English, Arabic, and various Arabic dialects
- **Voiceover Integration** - Upload your own voiceover or use AI-generated speech
- **Professional Video Output** - High-quality videos in multiple formats
- **Real-time Processing** - Fast scene generation and video rendering

### 🎨 **Video Creation Options**
- **Project Types**: Product or Service advertisements
- **Video Formats**: 16:9 (Landscape), 1:1 (Square), 9:16 (Portrait), or All Formats
- **Script Options**: Paste custom script or AI-generated content
- **Voiceover Options**: Upload audio, AI-generated, or no voiceover

### 🌍 **Language Support**
- **English** - Full Deepgram transcription support
- **Arabic Languages** - Advanced Aeneas integration for precise timing
  - Moroccan (Darija)
  - Algerian Arabic
  - Tunisian Arabic
  - Egyptian Arabic
  - Saudi Arabic
  - Gulf Arabic
  - Levantine Arabic

## 🚀 Getting Started

### **1. Create Your Account**
- Sign up for a free account
- No credit card required for trial
- Access to all basic features

### **2. Create Your First Project**
1. Click "Create New Video" on your dashboard
2. Choose project type (Product or Service)
3. Enter project details and description
4. Select video format(s)
5. Choose script option (paste or AI-generated)
6. Select voiceover option
7. Click "Generate My Video"

### **3. Review & Edit**
1. Review generated scenes
2. Regenerate individual scenes if needed
3. Upload custom media for specific scenes
4. Trim videos to perfect timing
5. Approve all scenes

### **4. Add Subtitles & Music**
1. Choose from 6 professional subtitle styles
2. Select background music (optional)
3. Preview your video
4. Generate final video

### **5. Download & Share**
1. Download your completed video
2. Share directly to social media
3. Use in your marketing campaigns
